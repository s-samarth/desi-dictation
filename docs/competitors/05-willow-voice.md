# Willow Voice

> **Layer:** Application (cross-platform cloud dictation, with opt-in offline) · **Verdict:** a YC-backed, fast-growing Wispr rival whose cross-platform reach (**including Android**) and enterprise logos make it a real medium-term threat on the platforms we must expand into.

## What it is

A direct Wispr Flow competitor from the current YC generation, differentiating on cross-platform reach, an opt-in offline mode, and a cleaner privacy posture (immediate deletion vs. Wispr's screenshot controversy). Growing fast with real enterprise logos and consumer ratings — the "younger, hungrier" version of the cloud-dictation category.

## Platforms & the India angle

Covers **all four platforms including Android**, which is our must-win gap. Its offline mode (Mac/iOS only) shows even cloud players feel the pull toward on-device. No Indian-language specialization surfaced; English is its strength (reviewers: 85–90% English accuracy, weaker on technical terms and non-English). So again: ahead of us on *platforms*, absent on *our market*.

## Features (exhaustive)

- Speech-to-text across Mac/Windows/iPhone/Android.
- **Style-matching across apps** (conversational in Slack, professional in client email).
- Automatic **filler-word removal** ("um," "like," "you know"); auto-formatting; grammar correction.
- **AI Mode**: transforms brief verbal notes into polished messages.
- **Offline Mode** (opt-in, Mac & iOS).
- Marketing: **200 ms latency**, "**3× higher accuracy than Apple's built-in dictation**."
- Team/Enterprise: SOC 2, HIPAA, zero data-retention (Enterprise).

## Strengths

Cross-platform including Android; fast-growing with social proof (ratings, enterprise logos, YC + notable angels); cleaner privacy story than Wispr (immediate deletion, offline option); crisp latency/accuracy marketing; enterprise-ready compliance early.

## Weaknesses

- Cloud-first by default — the privacy ceiling remains ("deleted after processing" still means it *left your device*; not good enough for Rohan/Priya/Suresh).
- English-centric; weaker on technical terms and non-English languages.
- Subscription-only at consumer tier (Indian payment-psychology friction).
- Young — limited long-term track record.

## Threat to us & how we differentiate

**Threat: medium, and rising on the platform axis.** Willow is exactly the kind of well-funded, cross-platform, fast-moving team that could add Indian languages and reach Android users before we do. Its "3× more accurate than Apple" positioning is the template we'd use, aimed at the same weak default. If VC dictation money turns toward India, Willow (or a Willow-like) is the shape it takes.

**We differentiate on:** true on-device (not "deleted after upload"), Hinglish depth, and India-native pricing/GTM. Against Willow specifically, our privacy claim is *categorically* stronger (nothing leaves vs. deleted-after), which matters most to the exact enterprise buyers (law/health/CA) Willow courts with SOC2/HIPAA — for those buyers, "there is nothing to retain" beats "we retain nothing."

**What to learn:** the "3× more accurate than Apple Dictation" side-by-side is the single best consumer marketing frame in this category — build our own Hinglish version (our Apex vs. Apple's English-mangling-Hindi, screenshotted). And their traction shows enterprise compliance logos convert even at the startup stage — our compliance one-pager (MONETIZATION.md) should exist early.
